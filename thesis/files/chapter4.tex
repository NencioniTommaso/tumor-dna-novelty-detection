% Separate data from the origin in feature space with maximum margin.
% Primal problem: $\min_{w, \rho, \xi} \frac{1}{2}\|w\|^2 - \rho + \frac{1}{\nu n} \sum_i \xi_i$
% subject to $\langle w, \Phi(x_i) \rangle \geq \rho - \xi_i$, $\xi_i \geq 0$.
%
% The $\nu$ parameter: upper bound on the fraction of outliers and lower bound
% on the fraction of support vectors.
